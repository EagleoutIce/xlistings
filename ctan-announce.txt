xlistings v1.1.0

This release adds code-link to the bundle. code-link collects definitions
while a document is typeset and links every occurrence of them in listings,
xlistings and minted code. It works without xlistings as well and has a
manual of its own, code-link-doc.pdf.

Changes to xlistings:
- the indentation in front of a highlighted segment (|warn|, |err|, |info|,
  ...) stays outside the highlight, also on further lines of a segment that
  spans several lines and next to escapes
- a digit after a letter stays part of the identifier (int32) instead of
  being highlighted as a number
- the thin space that opens inline code is dropped at the start of a
  paragraph; new \xlstInlineLead and \xlstInlineKeys
- the LaTeX language has better word boundaries (round-precision is one
  word) and the new key xlstcsbreak
- inline listings are more robust against escapes
