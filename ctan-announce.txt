xlistings v1.2.0

- Languages loaded on demand by a minted environment keep their macros and
  colours after it (#15).
- minted accepts language names without case and the usual Pygments short
  names (py, js, sh, tex, ...); new \xlstLanguageFileAlias adds more.
- An unknown language gives one error, its code is still typeset unhighlighted.
